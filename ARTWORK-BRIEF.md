# Artwork brief · Himanshi & Karan

The invitation website needs hand-painted illustrations like the ones in the reference video: realistic,
recognisable people, soft painted texture, warm light. This file lists every image the site needs, with a
prompt you can paste into an image tool.

## Where to make them

| Option | Good for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT (image generation)** | Best overall for likeness + consistent style | Upload your photos, paste the prompts below. Free tier has daily limits; Plus is faster. |
| **Google Gemini (image generation)** | Very good at keeping the same faces across many scenes | Upload photos the same way. |
| **Midjourney** | The most "painterly" finish | Use `--cref` with a character sheet for consistent faces. Paid. |
| **A wedding caricature studio** | Exactly the reference look, done for you | The studios watermarked in your video (e.g. Graphowl Studio, Studio Laali, wedding_caricature_art, graphicrafts26 on Instagram) make these. Costs more and takes 1–3 weeks. |

## How to keep every image looking like the same people (important)

1. **Make the character sheet first** (prompt 0). Upload 2–3 clear photos of each of you and of Ivy.
2. Pick the result you love, and **upload that character sheet with every later prompt**, saying
   "use these exact characters and this exact painting style".
3. Keep the **same chat/session** for all images.
4. Ask for **no text, no letters, no logos** in any image. The website adds all the words.
5. Size: **portrait 9:16 (1080 × 1920)** unless stated. Leave the **top 35% calm** (sky, wall, plain
   background) so the text can sit there.

**Style line to end every prompt with:**
> Hand-painted Indian wedding caricature illustration, soft gouache and watercolour texture, realistic
> proportions and likeness, warm natural light, gentle grain, elegant and cinematic, no text, no letters,
> no logos, portrait 9:16.

## 0 · Character sheet (make this first)

> Create a character sheet of this couple and their dog from the photos I've uploaded: full body, front
> and three-quarter views. **Him (Karan):** tall, athletic build, dark hair swept back, short full beard,
> rectangular dark-framed glasses. **Her (Himanshi):** long dark wavy hair, black rectangular glasses,
> slim. **Their dog (Ivy):** light golden-cream Indian mixed breed, floppy ears with tan tips, warm brown
> eyes, brown nose, blue collar. Plain warm-ivory background. *(+ style line, but landscape 16:9)*

## 1 · Opening: the door (two images)

**1a. Closed door**
> A grand carved wooden front door of an old Delhi family home, brass handles, a festive toran of
> small brass bells and fabric across the top, warm evening light, seen straight on, filling the frame.

**1b. Inside: Ivy waiting with the invitation** (this is where the invite begins)
> A cosy, warm Indian living room. Ivy (from the character sheet) sits proudly beside an antique wooden
> dresser, holding a cream envelope with a red wax seal in her mouth, wearing a cherry-red festive
> bandana and a small red tilak. On the dresser: a vintage radio and a cup of chai. On the wall above:
> a framed Shri Ganesh painting and a framed Ik Onkar. The upper half of the wall is plain warm peach so
> text can be placed there. Striped dhurrie on the floor.

## 2 · Our story: a storybook (8 images)

**2a. The book cover**
> Top-down view of a closed red cloth-bound hardcover book lying on a wooden desk, with a pencil,
> sticky notes, a tennis ball and a football-match ticket around it. The cover is blank (no text).
> *(16:9 landscape)*

**2b. Open book, blank pages**
> The same red book lying open on the desk, both cream pages completely blank, soft shadow in the
> spine. *(16:9 landscape)*: the website places each chapter picture on these pages.

**Chapter 1 · The Fixture**
> A youth football tournament on a sunny ground. Karan in a football kit, mid-match. At the sideline,
> Himanshi as the event organiser with a lanyard and a clipboard, looking at her phone. Bunting,
> spectators, goalposts.

**Chapter 2 · Pickup at Naraina Metro**
> Dusk in Delhi under the elevated Naraina Vihar metro line. A small red Hyundai i10 hatchback waits at
> the kerb with Karan at the wheel, smiling. Himanshi walks down the station steps towards the car,
> a little nervous, a little excited. Warm street lights, auto-rickshaws in the background.

**Chapter 3 · The food trail**
> Karan and Himanshi at a cosy restaurant table for two, bamboo steamers of momos between them, she has
> a cup of chai, he has a black coffee, both laughing. Warm pendant lights.

**Chapter 4 · Poles apart**
> A split scene down the middle. Left: Himanshi on a mountain balcony at night under a starry sky,
> with a book and a cup of tea, snowy peaks behind. Right: Karan on a beach at sunset with a coffee
> and a football at his feet. The two halves meet in the centre.

**Chapter 5 · The surprise in the book**
> Himanshi sitting on a sofa opening a wrapped book that Karan has just handed her; a small card has
> slipped out of its pages and she holds it, hand over her mouth, eyes wide. Karan kneels beside her
> holding an open ring box. Soft lamp light.

**Chapter 6 · Game, set, match**
> A blue hard tennis court with a green fence. Himanshi on one knee in a black-and-white striped sweater,
> white tennis skirt and black cap, holding up a ring. Karan in a dark hoodie and shorts with a blue
> racket backpack, laughing in surprise. Tennis balls lined up on the court in front of them.

**Finale**
> Karan and Himanshi walking hand in hand down a Delhi street at golden hour, Ivy trotting ahead on
> her blue lead, all three seen from behind.

## 3 · The four celebrations (4 images)

Leave the top 35% calm for the date, venue and dress code.

**Sagan & Cocktails · blue and silver**
> A glamorous cocktail night in a stylish club, palette of midnight blue, ice blue and silver: mirror
> balls, silver streamers, soft blue spotlights. Karan in a midnight-blue suit, Himanshi in a silver
> sequin saree, dancing; guests raising glasses in the background.

**Mehndi, Music & Masti**
> A lively night-time mehndi party on a terrace: string lights overhead, a dholak player and a
> harmonium player on a carpet, Himanshi having mehndi applied while friends dance around her.
> Emerald green, hot pink and gold.

**Haldi · lavender and yellow**
> A sunny morning courtyard, everyone in yellow and lavender. Karan and Himanshi seated on low wooden
> stools as family smear haldi on their cheeks, laughing. Brass bowls of turmeric, colourful fabric
> canopies overhead, bright and joyful.

**The Wedding · beige and cherry red**
> A royal day wedding in a palace courtyard with carved sandstone arches. Himanshi in a cherry-red
> bridal lehenga, Karan in a beige sherwani with a sehra, seated at the sacred fire beneath a cherry-red
> and gold mandap. Elders in the background, soft daylight, regal and calm.

## 4 · Monogram

Best done by a designer, but you can try:
> A luxury wedding monogram combining the letters H and K in an elegant engraved serif, with a fine
> crest around them that subtly includes a mountain peak (her) and a wave (him). Gold foil on ivory,
> centred, high resolution, square.
